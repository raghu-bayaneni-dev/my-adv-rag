# Q3 2026 Financial Performance & Budget Report (Confidential - Finance Department)

## 1. Executive Revenue Summary
During Q3 2026, the company generated **$48.5M in Total Revenue**, representing a 24% year-over-year growth.
- **Enterprise Subscriptions**: $36.2M (74.6% of total revenue)
- **Professional Services & Consulting**: $8.1M (16.7% of total revenue)
- **Marketplace & API Usage Fees**: $4.2M (8.7% of total revenue)

## 2. Departmental Operating Expenditures
- **R&D / Engineering**: $14.8M allocated across compute infrastructure and talent acquisition.
- **Sales & Marketing**: $18.4M allocated for global partner expansion.
- **General & Administrative**: $6.1M including legal, compliance, and real estate.

## 3. Discretionary Spending Approval Thresholds
Expenses exceeding **$50,000** require explicit sign-off from the VP of Finance. Expenses exceeding **$250,000** require Chief Financial Officer (CFO) and Board approval.
